Hi, I'm Young Bin,

I am a M.S. student at KAIST's Graduate School of Culture Technology, advised by Prof. Woontack Woo at the UVR Lab.

My research explores how to expand what objects can do within AR systems. Moving beyond simply reconstructing and tracking objects, I aim to connect their physical form with their function, enable users to interact with both physical and virtual objects, and ultimately create new forms of interaction by allowing the two to extend each other's possibilities.
