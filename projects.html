WISE UI Ongoing 2025
KAIST UVR Lab · Augmented Space-Time team
  • A platform that brings a shared UI to everyone using AR glasses. It reconstructs the surrounding space and the user’s location at a consistent level in any environment, estimates integrated attention from physical cues together with the user’s personal traits and nearby objects, and enables organic, personalized information augmentation and interaction across diverse spaces.

K-Culture Stress Hunters Course 2025 Fall
MV600 · Augmented Reality Project · KAIST
  • A K-Culture-based AR system for implicit stress relief through play. A three-stage embodied interaction (Affect Labeling, Externalization, and Relational Healing) lets users objectify negative emotions and resolve them through playful manipulation, drawing on Korean motifs (Moon Jar, folk paintings, Jeong 情). A pilot study showed increased positive affect and reduced state anxiety and stress. (First author)

ASL (American Sign Language)-based Shortcut Gesture for 3D Interaction Course 2026 Spring
GCT555 · 3D Interaction Design · KAIST
  • 2D keyboard shortcuts like Ctrl+C / V are universal, but 3D interfaces have no shared gesture vocabulary yet. Short-cut introduces a familiar, intuitive 3D shortcut system that maps Ctrl+C / F / Z / X directly to ASL finger-spelling gestures.
  • A camera tracks both hands in real time and recognizes Copy / Find / Undo / Delete (distinguishing left and right hands); users then select and place blocks in 3D space with a pinch interaction, with real-time visual feedback on match status.

AR Exhibition: Architectural Elements Augmentation Course 2025 Summer
CTP445 · Augmented Reality · KAIST
  • A spatial augmentation system for AR exhibitions that immersively reinterprets 2D artworks. Taking an artwork image (e.g., Raphael’s The School of Athens on an LED wall) as input, it generates floor patterns and architectural 3D models and semantically aligns them with the physical space, giving visitors the feeling of actually stepping inside the painting.
  • Course project that led to a poster at ISMAR 2025 (see Publications).
